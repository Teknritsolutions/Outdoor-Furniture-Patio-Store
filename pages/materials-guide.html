Natural materials Lasting comfort Considered design

01 / Solid wood

Teak: warmth that ages gracefully.

Dense natural grain and warm tones give teak its enduring appeal. Untreated teak gradually develops a silver patina outdoors.

  • Clean gently with mild soap and a soft brush.
  • Let the wood dry fully before covering.
  • Use a suitable teak protector to retain a warmer tone.

02 / Modern metal

Aluminum: light, clean, versatile.

Lightweight construction makes aluminum easy to rearrange. Powder-coated finishes add color and protect the surface.

Easy everyday care

Clean lines work well on contemporary terraces and compact balconies. Pair the frame with comfortable cushions and coordinated finishes for a setting that feels relaxed, practical, and easy to refresh.

Wash with a soft cloth and mild soap. Avoid abrasive cleaners; touch up scratches with the manufacturer’s recommended finish.

03 / Beautifully woven

Rattan: texture with a relaxed spirit.

Natural rattan is best kept in protected areas. Synthetic outdoor weave offers a similar tactile look with greater resistance to the weather.

Look after the weave

Brush away dust, wash synthetic weave gently, and keep natural rattan away from prolonged moisture.

04 / Crafted metal

Wrought iron: strength with character.

Classic lines, reassuring weight.

Decorative curves bring a timeless feel to courtyards and sheltered gardens. Pair the dark metal with soft seat cushions, natural stone, and generous planting to create a welcoming place to gather.

Wrought iron suits expressive garden settings and stays steady in breezy spaces. A sound protective finish is essential.

Check for chips, remove small rust spots promptly, and apply appropriate primer and touch-up paint. Store dry or use breathable covers.
